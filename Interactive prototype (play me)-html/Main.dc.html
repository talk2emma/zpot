Z-Trigger

Experimental · off by default

Zpot can help detect when you park, where your device supports this feature.

When your phone disconnects from your car’s Bluetooth and you start walking, Zpot can ask: “Did you just park here?”

Limitations

It can miss events or guess wrong. It never marks a space as free on its own, and Zpot works fully without it.
